# App Review Notes - Build 18

```md
Apple Health appears in the app UI in three places:

1. Settings > Integrations > Apple Health
   - Shows Apple Health status as Connected or Not connected.
   - Uses the subtitle: "Connect Apple Health to help personalise your supplement insights."

2. Settings > Connections > Apple Health
   - Shows the title "Apple Health" and status as Connected or Not connected.
   - Uses the description: "Suppro can read selected health and fitness data from the Apple Health app, with your permission."

3. Health tab
   - The Apple Health entry point is labelled before any permission request.
   - If the user taps connect, Suppro shows this message before the native permission prompt:
     "Suppro uses Apple Health data, such as activity and body measurements where available, to help personalise your insights. You can choose what to share and change permissions anytime in the Health app."
```
